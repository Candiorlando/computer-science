# Internal Product Architecture — Pathways Pro

**Internal product positioning and architecture reference. Do not publish this document or reproduce its detailed workflow descriptions on public-facing marketing pages.**

## What’s Inside

The platform is organized around the actual workflow of rehabilitation counseling, not a generic CRM bolted onto it:

### Case management core

Caseloads, case notes, case search, and document management built around how counselors actually track a client’s progress.

### Individualized Plans for Employment (IPEs)

Drafting and assessment tools aligned with WIOA requirements, not a blank text box.

### A business, vendor, and partner ecosystem

Portals that let agencies coordinate with employment partners, service vendors, and businesses offering supported and customized employment, all inside one system instead of email chains and spreadsheets.

### AI-assisted documentation

Drafting support for accommodation letters, clinical reports, and service plans, designed to give time back to counselors rather than replace their clinical judgment.

### Self-advocacy tools

Resources that help clients themselves understand and exercise their rights, not just professionals managing their case.

### Billing and scheduling

Subscription and invoicing support, plus calendar sync, so the administrative side of running a practice or program lives in the same place as the clinical side.
